About Wasabi

Wasabi opened its doors in 2000 and has become one of the premier Japanese restaurants in Ridgewood, New Jersey.

For the best sushi and Japanese cuisine in Bergen County, come to Wasabi. We are just a few blocks outside of downtown Ridgewood, and we offer full lunch and dinner menus six days a week.

For lunch, try a combination platter, noodle dish, rice bowl, or Sushi Sashimi. For dinner, choose from sushi rolls, hot and cold appetizers, teriyaki, tempura or Donburi.
